## VIII. CASE LAW SUPPORT

### Delaware Duty to Monitor (Caremark Standard)

In *In re Caremark International Inc. Derivative Litigation*, 698 A.2d 959 (Del. Ch. 1996), the court established that directors have a fiduciary duty to monitor corporate activities for fraud or illegal conduct. Failure to implement adequate monitoring systems can result in personal liability.

**Application:** Monitors, as court-appointed fiduciaries, have an even higher duty to monitor than corporate directors.

### False Claims Act "False Certification" Theory

Under the FCA, liability attaches when a funding recipient "expressly or implicitly certifies compliance with [civil rights laws] to obtain payment" while knowingly violating those laws. (See *United States ex rel. Hendow v. University of Phoenix*, 461 F.3d 1166 (9th Cir. 2006))

**Application:** If monitors received federal funds and certified oversight compliance, FCA liability applies.
